# Research methodology

## Claim ledger

List the claims that materially affect the conclusion. For each, record:

- claim type: direct fact, contested fact, inference, forecast, sentiment, or personal-case application;
- best source of truth;
- evidence opened in this run;
- disconfirming search performed;
- freshness and confidence;
- unresolved conflict.

## Evidence thresholds

- **Direct authoritative fact:** one current primary source may be sufficient when it directly owns the fact and there is no sign of dispute.
- **Contested or inferential claim:** require independent corroboration and an active search for evidence that would overturn it.
- **High-stakes rule, eligibility, rate, or deadline:** verify against the current authoritative text; use corroboration when interpretation or personal application is involved.
- **Forecast:** separate observations from assumptions and give scenarios rather than false precision.
- **Sentiment:** describe the sample, search terms, platform bias, and what was not observed.

Independence means sources do not merely repeat the same press release, dataset, or unnamed report.

## Source conflicts

Prefer direct/current/authoritative evidence, but do not hide a credible disagreement. Explain whether sources differ by date, jurisdiction, definition, sample, or interpretation. Lower confidence when the conflict cannot be resolved.

## Independent verification

For durable or high-stakes conclusions, give a fresh-context reviewer the claim ledger and opened sources. Ask it to find unsupported claims, stale facts, citation mismatches, and plausible disconfirming evidence. The reviewer checks the real sources, not the researcher's narrative.

## Completion

Research is complete when the answer's load-bearing claims meet their evidence thresholds and remaining uncertainty is explicit. It is `DEGRADED` when some required evidence or an independent check was unavailable but the load-bearing conclusion remains supported. It is `BLOCKED/NOT_PROVEN` when all load-bearing sources are unavailable or the conclusion cannot be checked; no substantive answer is supplied from memory.
