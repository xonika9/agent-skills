# Source and tool selection

Tool names change; inspect the current runtime before use.

## Preferred order

1. Primary source or authoritative registry/specification/filing.
2. Purpose-built connector, API, or CLI for structured or account-owned data.
3. Reputable independent reporting or research for context and corroboration.
4. Browser interaction when the source is visual, gated, or has no usable structured route.
5. Social/community material for sentiment and firsthand reports, clearly labeled as such.

Search-result snippets are discovery aids, not evidence. Open the source page or document.

## Browser

Follow `x9-browser-session` before browser work; it owns controller selection, task-tab isolation, private-surface safety, and browser-route failure.

## Social search

- State platform, time window, query terms, and sorting method.
- Sample both supporting and opposing posts when testing a contested claim.
- Distinguish reach or repetition from independent agreement.
- Do not infer population sentiment from an unrepresentative feed.

### X / Twitter

Use `x9-browser-session` and the user's authenticated browser for X/Twitter research. Open-web search may locate candidate posts, but inspect the original posts, threads, replies, and quote posts in the X interface before using them as evidence. This keeps login-gated and account-visible context available and prevents search snippets from standing in for the source.

The account, locale, follows, and platform ranking can shape what is visible. Prefer targeted searches over the home feed, record the query and ordering, and disclose this personalization when it limits the conclusion.

Search deliberately instead of scrolling an algorithmic feed blindly:

- use X advanced search or equivalent search operators for accounts, exact phrases, date windows, language, links, engagement, replies, and exclusions;
- compare `Latest` and `Top` when both views are available and record which ordering was sampled;
- open relevant threads in full and inspect replies and quote posts for counter-evidence;
- record the author, post date, query/window, and why the account is informative;
- treat engagement as reach, not independent agreement, and corroborate load-bearing factual claims off-platform.

### Reddit and forums

Use open-web discovery such as `site:reddit.com <query>` when native search is weak, then open and read the original thread. Sample more than one community or thread when making a broad claim, note dates and obvious selection bias, and inspect disagreement rather than quoting only the most upvoted answer.

Reddit and forums are useful for lived experience, recurring complaints, workarounds, and vocabulary. They are signals, not authoritative proof; verify any load-bearing factual claim against a source that owns the fact.

## Freshness

Capture publication/effective dates and the date accessed. Re-check volatile tables, tool lists, prices, versions, schedules, and policies on every substantive use.

## Completion

Social or community evidence records its platform, time window, query terms, ordering, sample limits, and observed disagreement. X/Twitter evidence comes from opened original posts through the authenticated route; Reddit or forum evidence comes from opened original threads. Corroborate every load-bearing factual claim with a source that owns the fact.
